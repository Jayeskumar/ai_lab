# Volume 1 — Foundations and Alternative Mathematical Objects {.unnumbered}

The first volume builds quantity from nothing but symbols and rules. Chapter 2
shows that counting is the complete invariant of free rearrangement and that
modular arithmetic is what a single relation does to counting. Chapter 3 lets
place value emerge from local exchanges of tokens. Chapters 4 and 5 study the
transformation systems themselves: when they stop, how long they take, and
which games they define.
