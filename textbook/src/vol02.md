# Volume 2 — Invented Operations and Their Properties {.unnumbered}

Here a single binary operation is invented and nothing about it is assumed:
every law is a hypothesis. Chapter 6 maps which laws have finite models of
which sizes; Chapter 7 anchors an operation to a successor and finds that the
sizes admitting a model form arithmetic sets — powers of two for
left-distributivity (Laver's tables), the odd numbers, and the moduli in which
the golden ratio exists.
