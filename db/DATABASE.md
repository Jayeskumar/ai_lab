- **agent:** Mathematical Explorer
- **question:** In System A with the swap relations xy <-> yx, what distinguishes two words that cannot be transformed into each other?
- **hypothesis:**

  Two words are congruent iff, for every symbol s, deleting all other symbols gives the same word s...s (equal symbol projections). The shortlex-completed system {yx -> xy : x < y} is finite and its normal forms are the sorted words.
